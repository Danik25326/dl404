About

A company of one person.
Who cannot be found.

DL404 is the umbrella for everything built, discovered, or composed by Danylo — developer, mathematician, and musician based in Kharkiv, Ukraine.

The name says it: we're not where you expect to find us. No corporate hallways, no investor decks. Just work that matters.

Every project under DL404 is made by one person who cares too much to ship something average.
